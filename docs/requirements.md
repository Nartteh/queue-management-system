# Requirements

## Users
- Customer: picks a service and gets a ticket
- Staff: calls the next customer and updates their status
- Admin: manages services, counters, and reports

## Features
- Create and list services
- Issue tickets (e.g. F-001) and show people ahead
- Call next, mark served, skipped, or cancelled
- Customer page, staff page, and public display board
- Staff and admin login
- Priority tickets and estimated waiting time
- Daily reports

## Sample institution
KNUST Finance Office with services: Fees Payment, Clearance, Enquiries
